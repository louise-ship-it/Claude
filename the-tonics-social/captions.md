## Tips
- Pinned first comment: "Where should we play next? 👇"
- Post Thu–Sun evenings, when people are planning nights out.
- Tag the venue and anyone in the band so it reaches their followers too.
- Use the cover image (The_Tonics_cover_9x16.jpg) as the Reel/TikTok thumbnail.
- If the song is a cover, check the platform's music-rights rules. Some platforms mute
  or block cover performances, and adding the song title can help them match it to a licence.
